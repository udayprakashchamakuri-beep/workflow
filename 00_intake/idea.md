# Website Idea

Project name:
- Syllabus Sprint

One-line idea:
- A website for students that lets users upload a syllabus and get an AI study plan.

Problem being solved:
- Students struggle to turn long syllabus documents into a clear week-by-week study plan.

Target users:
- Students preparing for assignments, quizzes, and exams.

Core features:
- Upload a syllabus
- Extract important dates and topics
- Generate a study plan
- Show a weekly study schedule

Differentiator:
- Turn one syllabus upload into a simple study plan instead of forcing students to organize everything manually.

Anything already decided:
- Milestone 01 starts with a polished front-end foundation and study-plan preview.
